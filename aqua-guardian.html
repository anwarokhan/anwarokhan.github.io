Prototype overview

Aqua Guardian is Atholton Electronics Club’s water-quality buoy project. Its assemblies include an enclosure, solar power, sensor connections, and cellular telemetry. I lead the mechanical work.

Build documentation

The photographs show enclosure assembly, internal wiring, circuit boards, and field handling. The winter clips were recorded in February 2026. The complete team submission film appears below.

Test status · October 2026

Following a short October 24 test, the buoy was removed for changes. Further deployment and independent sensor comparisons remain work to complete. The prototype is not a certified environmental measurement system.
