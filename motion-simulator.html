Hardware record

The simulator combines a cockpit frame, steering and pedal controls, displays, motor-drive electronics, and a motion-platform development project.

Motion-model testing · April 2025

The dated build log records homing tests, Hall-sensor troubleshooting, gear slippage, and SimTools configuration. The photographs include printed joints, gearboxes, and a tubing test assembly.

Frame and controls

Later photographs show an aluminum-extrusion cockpit, a triple-monitor structure, a drive enclosure, and wiring. The axis-test video is linked below.

Configuration note · October 2026

The current design direction is a three-degree-of-freedom platform with four SFX-100-style linear actuators. Earlier six-axis CAD models and test assemblies remain in the gallery as dated development records. The project is ongoing.
