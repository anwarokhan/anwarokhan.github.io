A graph someone could use

I started working on a program to display Enphase solar data in graphs and tables, looking at seasons, time of day, and conditions affecting energy collection. Python’s hvPlot library gave me a way to turn the data into something people could explore.

Working with what was available

The project log records that I could not obtain API access in time for the demonstration. I used our family’s historical solar data instead, looking at seasonal effects, foliage, and times of peak collection.

The family helped run the Maker Faire stand. Explaining a graph to someone stopping at the booth asked something different of me than getting the code to run.

A note on the dates

The detailed project page records preparation and a June 1, 2024 event. A separate recap carries a June 2023 publication date. I preserve both original records without treating that disagreement as a verified event date. Live API integration is described as a future plan.
